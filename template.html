Nov 21 · the public day

A queue on a residential block needs a plan. Here's ours.

Both houses sit on quiet residential streets. Timed ticketing has worked well at other public activations in our houses, and it's also what keeps the owners and neighbours on side.

Parking permits, if you need them: Cocoon handles the application. Similar activations have run about $2,500 for the whole booking, but the Mayor's Office sets the final cost once we submit the size of the permitted area.
